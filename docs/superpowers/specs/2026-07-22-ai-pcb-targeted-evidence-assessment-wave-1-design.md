# AI PCB Targeted Evidence Assessment Wave 1 Design

## Scope

Assess only the ten successfully normalized Wave 1 records associated with `PCB-ER-A01`, `A02`, `A03`, `A04`, `B01`, and `B02`. The assessment is offline and read-only with respect to acquisition, cognition, gap review and Gate artifacts. Blocked, failed, preflight and normalization-failed records are governance context only and cannot become claim evidence.

## Architecture

Create one integrated assessment artifact using schema version 2.7.0 and one deterministic Markdown projection. A focused assessment module validates upstream hashes, reconstructs Wave evidence chains, validates v2.3-artifact-to-legacy-normalized lineage by raw content hash, validates section locators, computes atomic-claim grounding constraints and derives ER status from claim status without permitting claim sufficiency to auto-promote an ER.

## Evidence semantics

Every claim records explicit scope, denominator, evidence stance, evidence chains, freshness and cognition ceiling. Exact duplicate Wave records collapse into one chain. A02 OIF documents remain provisionally dependent unless the text establishes different data/method provenance. B01 Isola records remain one supplier chain, and the encrypted PDF remains non-evidence. Unknown publication dates cap freshness at `unknown` and prevent unconditional high confidence.

## Outputs

- `artifacts/research_projects/v2_1/analysis/ai_pcb_targeted_evidence_assessment_wave_1_v1.json`
- `artifacts/research_projects/v2_1/reports/ai_pcb_targeted_evidence_assessment_wave_1_v1.md`

The JSON artifact is the sole assessment fact source. The report is byte-stable UTF-8 Markdown generated by a fixed renderer and verified by hash and byte comparison.

## Safety

Validators reject unapproved ERs, invalid or drifted locators, non-normalized evidence, duplicate-chain inflation, supplier-chain inflation, denominator-free sufficiency, certain freshness from unknown-date evidence, downstream mechanism/causal/bottleneck/value/company objects and any authorization of Wave 1b, Stage A2 or Stage B.
