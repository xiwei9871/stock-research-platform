# AI PCB Evidence Gap Review and Targeted Research Design v1

This stage is an offline research-design exercise. It reads the frozen cognition package, audit, report and acquisition checkpoint by immutable hash and does not acquire, reassess or promote evidence.

## Research sequence

The design separates four levels:

```text
signal/material mechanism
→ manufacturing difficulty or bounded capability
→ qualified effective capacity
→ commercial shortage and value migration
```

Evidence from one level cannot establish the next. Technical standards and measurements can establish definitions and mechanisms. Manufacturing-process, reliability and test data are required for manufacturing difficulty. Denominator-defined yield, qualification, mix, utilization and output are required for effective capacity. Matched demand, duration, price, cost and profit-allocation evidence would be separately required for commercial bottlenecks or value migration.

## Public evidence limits

Availability fields are design judgments, not claims that a document exists. Yield and effective capacity remain `structurally_limited` because product mix, internal process windows, customer qualification and qualified output may not be independently public. Repeated searching does not override that ceiling.

## Evidence independence

URL count and file format do not define independence. A vendor page, PDF, news release and secondary article repeating the same underlying data remain one evidence chain. Each ER defines its minimum independent-chain count and whether a supplier-independent source is required.

## Stopping rules

Research may end as resolved, bounded by public evidence, structurally limited, redundant or out of scope. Stopping is not automatically resolution. Product generation, application, method, geography, observation period and denominator must remain comparable.

## Formal outputs

- The JSON artifact is the sole research-design fact source.
- The Markdown report is a deterministic renderer projection.
- Future acquisition, Stage A2, Stage B, bottleneck judgment, value-migration judgment and company mapping remain unauthorized.
